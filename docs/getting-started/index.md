# Getting started

NetRoll runs amateur-radio **nets** — coordinated on-air meetups where stations check in,
exchange signal reports, and follow a net-control-moderated agenda. This section takes you from
nothing to a working account and your first net, in about ten minutes total.

## The path

**1. [Create an account](create-an-account.md)** — magic-link sign-in, terms, and your callsign.
Everyone starts here. ~3 minutes.

Then take whichever branch matches you:

**2a. [Run your first net](run-your-first-net.md)** — create a net, take it live, and log a
station checking in. For net owners and net control stations. ~5 minutes.

**2b. [Join a net](join-a-net.md)** — find a running net, watch the roster update live, and
check yourself in. For participants. ~3 minutes.

Watching a net needs no account at all. If that's all you're after, skip step 1 and go straight
to [join a net](join-a-net.md).

## What each step unlocks

| You want to | Account | Callsign |
|-------------|:-------:|:--------:|
| Browse and watch live nets | — | — |
| Favorite a net, use **My Nets** | ✅ | — |
| Check yourself in | ✅ | ✅ |
| Own and run a net | ✅ | ✅ |

## Where to go next

Once you've run or joined a net:

- **[Nets](../nets/index.md)** — schedule a net, control who can find it, add co-owners.
- **[Live sessions](../live-sessions/index.md)** — the full operating console: check-ins,
  roster, roles, handoff, exports.
- **[Your account](../account/index.md)** — change your email, connect QRZ, export your data.

## Learn the vocabulary

NetRoll uses net terminology throughout, and a few words mean something specific inside the app
— a *net definition* is not the same thing as a *net session*.

- **[Glossary](../reference/glossary.md)** — every term, including the NetRoll-specific ones.
- **[Session lifecycle](../live-sessions/session-lifecycle.md)** — what scheduled, live, paused,
  and closed actually mean.
