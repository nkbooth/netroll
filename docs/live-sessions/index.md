# Live sessions

A *session* is one live occurrence of a net. It snapshots the definition when it starts,
records everything that happens as an ordered event log, and streams that log to every
connected viewer in real time. This section is the operating console: what you do while the net
is on the air.

## Start here

- **[Start and close a session](start-and-close-a-session.md)** — take a net live, retune a
  way in mid-net, and close it with a summary.

- **[Log check-ins](log-check-ins.md)** — the quick-add hot path, signal reports, staying

- **[Work the roster](work-the-roster.md)** — the working-station cursor, rounds, notes, and

- **[Grant roles and relays](grant-roles-and-relays.md)** — bring in loggers and relay stations

- **[Export a log](export-a-log.md)** — CSV and ADIF downloads after the net.
