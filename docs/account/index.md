# Your account

NetRoll identifies you by email and has no passwords. Your callsign is a separate, reserved
identifier that gates self-check-in and net ownership.

## Guides

- **[Sign in](sign-in.md)** — magic links, sessions, and changing your identifying email.
- **[Reserve a callsign](reserve-a-callsign.md)** — format rules, uniqueness, and changing it.
- **[Connect QRZ](connect-qrz.md)** — store QRZ credentials so check-in lookups use your
  subscription.
- **[Export or delete your data](export-or-delete-your-data.md)** — data export, deletion, and
  the undelete window.

## What each thing unlocks

| Capability | Needs |
|------------|-------|
| Browse, filter, and view live sessions | Nothing |
| Favorite nets, "My nets" | An account |
| Check yourself in | A verified email and a callsign |
| Own and run a net | A verified email and a callsign |
